Our story

Same street, same name, since 1987

Gabrclean opened on Abdel Moneim Riad Street in Maadi in 1987 and has been there ever since. Generations of Maadi families have come through our door, and our name still hangs in brass above it.

Our new logo is drawn from that same sign, with the same letters and dots, updated for today.
